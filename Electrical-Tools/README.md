# Tile Cutter

This is my first exploration for Inside-Devices.

I opened this electrical tile cutter out of curiosity to explore its internal components, understand how its parts are arranged, and learn how the machine works.

## What I Explored

- Opened the tile cutter to look at its internal construction.
- Observed the different components and parts.
- Explored how the electrical and mechanical parts are arranged.
- Tried to understand how the different parts work together.
- Recorded interesting observations and discoveries.
